PITCH C: The keys held. The approvals did not.

ARGUMENT: In three thefts over 20 months, TraderTraitor broke no cryptography. It made each exchange's own approval process sign. Bybit lost about $1.46bn on 21 February 2025 [https://www.elliptic.co/blog/bybit-hack-largest-in-history] through a Safe{Wallet} developer's compromised machine [https://x.com/safe/status/1894768522720350673]. Bitget lost $387.5m on 24 September 2026 through altered transaction data; its keys were untouched, Wikipedia reports [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitget]. On 29 October 2026 Binance lost about $6.4bn when valid EIP-7702 authorizations let attacker code run its hot wallet [https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/binance-exploit-latest-findings-and-response/] [https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7702].

WHY NOW: It is the largest crypto theft on record. Chainalysis reports $2.8bn frozen, about 44% of the total, mostly stablecoins. The bitcoin and ether stay out of reach.

READER LEARNS: The working defense is an issuer's freeze button, and attackers now convert to unfreezable assets within minutes.

SOURCES: FBI PSA I-022625-PSA, primary [https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2025/PSA250226]; Safe's own statement, primary; Chainalysis on-chain tracing; Elliptic; EIP-7702 specification, primary; Wikipedia, secondary and disclosed [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Binance_hack].

UNCERTAIN: The attribution is an analytics-firm assessment. The FBI has named TraderTraitor only for Bybit. Binance's laptop explanation is preliminary.
