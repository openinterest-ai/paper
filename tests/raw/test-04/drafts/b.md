LEAD: Gitcoin contributors contest a crowdfunding partnership. [https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/25430]
TEST EDITION | 29 October 2026

A 29 October 2026 reply makes ownership a condition of Gitcoin's proposed crowdfunding partnership with Bread Cooperative. [https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/25430/10] An earlier critic questions whether the pilot serves Gitcoin's local-first social ambitions. [https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/25430/9]

Kevin Owocki's proposal offers three partnership paths, from a pilot to adopting the platform as a core Gitcoin product. [https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/25430/1] The pilot would combine a $10,000–$50,000 development grant with $100,000–$250,000 in withdrawable principal. [https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/25430/1] Those amounts describe proposed spending and deposits, rather than an approved allocation. [https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/25430/1]

In a 27 October 2026 reply, thedevanshmehta opposes the pilot and asks why Gitcoin cannot use Giveth's existing platform. [https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/25430/9] The contributor also doubts that Bread's audience would extend beyond the same online community. [https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/25430/9]

On 29 October 2026, TerexitariusStomp answers by demanding clearer ownership terms for the proposed production partnership. [https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/25430/10] The contributor wants copyleft code and Gitcoin-controlled contracts written into the agreement. [https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/25430/10] That reply proposes testing donor retention, matching efficiency and participation from donors arriving through fiat payments. [https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/25430/10]

Owocki's original proposal already leaves intellectual property, contract control and withdrawal arrangements for negotiation before money moves. [https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/25430/1] It also leaves the metrics governing expansion beyond the pilot open for discussion. [https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/25430/1] The disagreement therefore concerns both the product's purpose and the conditions for funding it. [https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/25430/9] [https://gov.gitcoin.co/t/25430/10]
