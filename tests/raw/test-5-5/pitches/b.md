# Pitch B: The stablecoin rulebook arrives, and the largest issuer is not obviously inside it

ARGUMENT: On 23 October 2026 the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency published its rule implementing the GENIUS Act, effective 18 January 2027 [https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/10/23/2026-21706/implementing-the-guiding-and-establishing-national-innovation-for-us-stablecoins-act-for-the]. The piece will argue that the rule governs national-bank-chartered issuers first, while the market's largest dollar token, USDT, sits outside that channel. DefiLlama showed USDT at about $179.6 billion and USDC at about $72.0 billion on 31 October 2026 [https://stablecoins.llama.fi/stablecoins]. The feature will test whether the rule changes who can hold that share, or only who may market to US users.

WHY NOW: The rule is eight days old, and the effective date is under three months away. No prior Open Interest edition has led with it.

THE READER LEARNS: which issuers fall under which regulator, what reserve and disclosure duties apply, and what stays unknown until other agencies finish their rules.
