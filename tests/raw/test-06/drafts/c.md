LEAD:
Binance schedules withdrawals before completing asset replacement. [https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/73911fb819b240f88b88256bdd318eda]

Binance's 1 November 2026 notice sets a phased withdrawal restart after the exchange's $6.3 billion theft. [https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/73911fb819b240f88b88256bdd318eda] [https://www.binance.com/en/square/post/383048947006139] The company says it has replaced 95% of affected assets, with less liquid tokens still outstanding. [https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/73911fb819b240f88b88256bdd318eda]

Binance scheduled the first withdrawal phase for 1 November 2026, between 06:00 and 09:00 UTC. Its notice lists fifteen networks, including Bitcoin, Ethereum and Solana, with specified assets on each. Other network-and-asset combinations would follow when ready; the notice gives no dates for those additions. [https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/73911fb819b240f88b88256bdd318eda]

The distinction is between money earmarked and tokens replaced. On 30 October 2026, Binance said it had reserved more than $6.34 billion, including $5 billion in affected tokens. It promised to absorb the financial loss rather than pass it to customers. [https://www.binance.com/en/square/post/383048947006139]

Its 31 October 2026 notice names three funding sources: its emergency user-protection reserve, part of its futures insurance fund and corporate treasury. Binance says futures insurance money belongs to the company, rather than customers. It also says those insurance balances do not appear in customer proof-of-reserves checks. [https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/1fdf1f61559c401da0435895ffcccb62]

The company dates its new reserve snapshot to 31 October 2026 at 00:00 UTC. It estimates publication of the full cryptographic proof by 3 November 2026. That remains a company timetable, rather than a completed verification reported here. [https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/1fdf1f61559c401da0435895ffcccb62]

TRM Labs attributes the theft to North Korea's TraderTraitor group and assesses Binance's signing infrastructure as the likely point of compromise. Those are the investigator's conclusions. Binance's reopening notice promises a post-incident report when its investigation concludes, leaving the final explanation outstanding. [https://www.trmlabs.com/resources/blog/north-korea-steals-usd-6-36-billion-from-binance-in-the-largest-hack-in-history] [https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/73911fb819b240f88b88256bdd318eda]

[NEWS] Withdrawal requests for zero empty b14g staking orders. [https://www.bitquery.io/investigations/b14g-hack]

Bitquery's Gaurav Agarwal reports that an attacker emptied 91 b14g staking orders on Core on 31 October 2026. [https://www.bitquery.io/investigations/b14g-hack] His investigation values the 97.05 million CORE taken at $1.47 million at the pre-theft price, rather than $97 million. [https://www.bitquery.io/investigations/b14g-hack]

The traced transaction requested zero CORE; Core's staking code interpreted zero as the entire stake, according to Agarwal. [https://www.bitquery.io/investigations/b14g-hack] [https://scan.coredao.org/tx/0xe1947f325e1f0bc22290ea97dc5b2ce44827fbb56e1cc40abaeda1d2ed04f62c]

Alexander Gurevich appeared in federal court on 30 October 2026 after extradition from Israel, the Justice Department announced. [https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/israeli-national-extradited-face-charges-stealing-nearly-3m-virtual-currency-heist] Prosecutors allege four unauthorized Nomad Bridge transactions on 1 August 2022 took assets then worth $2.89 million. The charges include computer damage and money laundering; the indictment contains allegations, and Gurevich remains presumed innocent. [https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/israeli-national-extradited-face-charges-stealing-nearly-3m-virtual-currency-heist]

TECH:

[TECH] An OP Stack prototype aggregates post-quantum authentication proofs. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26208]

Researcher sm-stack published an experimental EIP-8288 adaptation for OP Stack on 2 November 2026. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26208] The design separates signatures from transaction data and posts an aggregate proof covering multiple blocks to Ethereum. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26208]

The author reports up to 170 leanSPHINCS signatures proved per second on a 32-core machine using two-second blocks. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26208] The prototype excludes prover operating costs from its fee mechanism, leaving a deployment cost unresolved. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26208]

[TECH] Dan Guo separates same-block quotes from evidence of speed. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26201]

Dan Guo's 31 October 2026 study examines 92,627 trade executions across nine Ethereum venues with prices controlled by individual market makers. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26201] The sample covers 10 September 2026 through 9 October 2026. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26201]

Guo reports that three venues require quote updates stamped with the executing block's timestamp, making same-block pairing a contract rule. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26201] Maker signing times remain unobservable, and some venue implementations remain unverified, limiting what the findings establish about speed. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26201]

[CULTURE] Molly White puts token recovery against immutability. [https://www.citationneeded.news/its-always-north-korea/]

White's 30 October 2026 essay argues that centralized stablecoin issuers abandoned early crypto ideals of decentralization and immutability. [https://www.citationneeded.news/its-always-north-korea/] She identifies their ability to stop stolen tokens and issue replacements as the practical consequence. [https://www.citationneeded.news/its-always-north-korea/]

She distinguishes those powers from protocol freezes that may immobilize funds without returning them to victims. [https://www.citationneeded.news/its-always-north-korea/]
