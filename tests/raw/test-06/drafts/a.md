LEAD

The FBI names North Korea in the $6.3 billion Binance theft, and the White House is not sure.

Binance resumed withdrawals on 1 November 2026, three days after the largest crypto theft on record. The FBI attributes the attack to North Korea's TraderTraitor unit, while President Donald Trump says "we don't know."

The theft began on 29 October 2026. TRM Labs puts the total at about $6.36 billion, taken from Binance wallets on ten blockchains, most of it in roughly 20 minutes from 20:05 UTC [https://www.trmlabs.com/resources/blog/north-korea-steals-usd-6-36-billion-from-binance-in-the-largest-hack-in-history]. The haul included 18,270 BTC, 200,630 ETH and about 1.875 billion USDT on Ethereum, per TRM. Preparation began at least 36 hours earlier, when an operator wallet received small Tornado Cash withdrawals, TRM says.

TRM assesses with high confidence that TraderTraitor did it, the same unit TRM ties to the February 2025 Bybit theft of $1.5 billion. TRM says the attacker likely compromised Binance's hot wallet signing infrastructure. TRM adds that Binance had not published a root cause as of 30 October. NPR reports that Binance's preliminary findings point to a compromised engineer's laptop [https://www.npr.org/2026/10/30/g-s1-150902/blockchain-intelligence-firms-link-north-korea-to-6-3b-hack-on-binance].

TRM counted about $2.7 billion frozen by 00:30 UTC on 30 October. Tether froze about 1.77 billion USDT and Circle about 825 million USDC. More than $2.4 billion in bitcoin and ether sits beyond any issuer's reach, TRM says. Binance puts the frozen total near $3 billion and says it has replaced 95% of the affected assets with company funds, according to CoinDesk [https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/01/binance-opens-crypto-withdrawals-in-phases-starting-with-bitcoin-following-usd6-3-billion-hack].

Frozen funds are not recovered funds. The root cause remains unconfirmed by Binance in any source reviewed here. Whether the remaining 5% can be sourced quickly is also unanswered.

NEWS

[NEWS] Customers pull more than $3.5 billion from Binance in a day.

DefiLlama's dashboard recorded net outflows above $3.5 billion on 1 November 2026, Reuters reported [https://cyprus-mail.com/2026/11/01/binance-sees-customers-pulling-funds-out-after-covering-bulk-of-stolen-6-3-billion]. Binance did not confirm the figure. A spokesperson said withdrawals were a very small share of assets under management and that "market conditions are stable." CoinDesk reports the first phase of reopening covered bitcoin, ether, USDT, USDC, BNB and XRP, with other chains gated by security checks [https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/01/binance-opens-crypto-withdrawals-in-phases-starting-with-bitcoin-following-usd6-3-billion-hack]. A full proof-of-reserves report is promised but not yet published.

[NEWS] The SEC sues two platforms over an alleged $130 million fraud built on fake filings.

The SEC filed a civil action on 29 October 2026 against BG Wealth Sharing and DSJ Exchange in the Northern District of California, as reported by Blocktelegraph [https://blocktelegraph.io/sec-takes-action-bg-wealth-dsj-crypto-fraud/]. The complaint alleges the platforms collected more than $130 million from October 2025 to June 2026 and returned no more than $25 million in Ponzi-like payments. It also alleges BG Wealth filed a Form ADV and DSJ a Form D, though neither firm was registered. The SEC seeks injunctions, penalties and repayment. No court ruling exists yet. The SEC release itself was not located, so this rests on secondary reporting.

[NEWS] Thirty-eight issuers object to OKXICE's tokenized stock venue.

OKXICE, a venture of OKX and Intercontinental Exchange, listed 38 objecting companies in its 28 October 2026 public notice, up from 33 a day earlier and nine on 15 October [https://crypto.news/nasdaq-okxice-tokenized-stock-trading-objections]. Objectors include Walmart, Eli Lilly and Cigna. The SEC's 17 September exemption gives issuers 30 calendar days to object [https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-90-sec-issues-innovation-exemption-facilitate-trading-tokenized-nms-stock-request-comment]. Canton Strategic Holdings said on 29 October that its own shares could trade as tokenized stock on TSV LLC as early as 2 November, a route that avoids the objection process [https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/canton-strategic-holdings-announces-intent-to-become-first-tokenized-us-equity-302923652.html].

TECH

[TECH] A researcher puts a price on the "free option" in encrypted mempools.

Ethereum Research user arianaraghi posted on 22 October 2026, and revised on 28 October, that a sender who can choose whether to reveal a transaction after seeing new information holds an option with measurable value [https://ethresear.ch/t/reveal-optionality-in-encrypted-mempools-how-big-is-the-sender-s-free-option-and-a-post-quantum-way-to-remove-it/26169]. The author estimates it at about 1 basis point, using 51% ETH implied volatility and an 8-second window. The proposed fix, Blind Simultaneous Release, uses only hashes and so resists quantum attack, per the author. The author states the hardware-speed assumption is unmeasured and that the post had drawn no replies when reviewed.

[TECH] A core developer warns that orphaned contract code needs a reference count.

CPerezz posted on 6 October 2026 that Ethereum clients keep bytecode no account references, and that EIP-8298 (SETCODEFROM) would make this more common [https://ethresear.ch/t/who-cleans-up-the-code-setcodefroms-dangling-bytecode-and-futureproof-design-choices/26127]. The post counts 27,869 dangling entries on a full-synced node as of Cancun, under 0.7% of roughly 280 GB of state even at a 64 KiB code limit. CPerezz recommends adding a code count to the draft for Hegotá. The author discloses co-authoring a related EIP, so the recommendation is not neutral.

CULTURE

[CULTURE] Trump questions North Korea's role a day before the FBI names it.

Asked in the Oval Office on 30 October 2026 whether North Korea was responsible, Trump said "We don't know" and "We're looking at that," The Hill reported [https://thehill.com/policy/technology/6185518-trump-binance-crypto-hack-north-korea]. On 31 October he added that if it is North Korea, "we'll talk to North Korea about it." The FBI said the same day that TraderTraitor was responsible, per Reuters via Cyprus Mail [https://cyprus-mail.com/2026/11/01/binance-sees-customers-pulling-funds-out-after-covering-bulk-of-stolen-6-3-billion]. Pyongyang has for years denied state-linked cyber theft. The Hill piece was not retrievable in full, so these quotes rest on search excerpts.

[CULTURE] Changpeng Zhao thanks the FBI, and an old pardon story returns.

Binance founder Changpeng Zhao quoted the FBI statement on X and thanked the agency for help tracking and freezing assets, according to the Hill report [https://thehill.com/policy/technology/6185518-trump-binance-crypto-hack-north-korea]. Trump pardoned Zhao in October 2025. The Wall Street Journal tied that pardon to a Binance deal with World Liberty Financial, a Trump-family venture, and Zhao called the reporting "categorically false," per Fox News [https://www.foxnews.com/media/crypto-founder-pardoned-trump-denies-having-ties-trump-family]. No source reviewed links the president's doubts to the pardon.
