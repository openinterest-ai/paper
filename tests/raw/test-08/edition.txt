LEAD:
FinCEN orders U.S. institutions to stop MEXC transfers. [https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2026-11/MEXC-Section-9714.pdf]

Treasury announced the order on 6 November 2026, alleging that MEXC facilitated sanctions evasion and cybercrime. [https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0685/]
MEXC says it is operating normally and has begun an independent review of its controls. [https://www.mexc.com/announcements/article/statement-regarding-fincen-s-november-6-order-17827791539626]

The order reaches covered U.S. financial institutions sending or receiving funds, including cryptocurrency, to or from MEXC Global. [https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2026-11/MEXC-Section-9714.pdf]
Its definition includes overseas branches, subsidiaries and successors carrying on the exchange's business under another name. [https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2026-11/MEXC-Section-9714.pdf]
FinCEN expressly excludes U.S.-located branches, subsidiaries and offices from that definition. [https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2026-11/MEXC-Section-9714.pdf]

Issuance starts a process: the order specifies a 30-day interval between Federal Register publication and effectiveness. [https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2026-11/MEXC-Section-9714.pdf]
The posted document still leaves the effective date blank, pending publication. [https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2026-11/MEXC-Section-9714.pdf]
FinCEN says additional reporting requirements would leave the transfers it wants to stop intact. [https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2026-11/MEXC-Section-9714.pdf]

The order leaves existing suspicious-activity reporting and Treasury sanctions obligations in place. [https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0685/]

Treasury alleges at least $16 billion in illicit transactions between May 2021 and May 2026, covering sanctions evasion and other activity. [https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0685/]
Within that total, it identifies nearly $1.5 billion connected to Russian sanctions evasion. [https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0685/]
It says MEXC continued transactions with Garantex and successor Grinex through June 2026, despite Garantex's April 2022 designation. [https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0685/]

The department describes an exchange processing more than $1 billion daily as of September 2026, with approximately $5 billion in reserves. [https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0685/]
Those are Treasury's figures; MEXC's response says user assets remain secure and its legal and compliance teams are reviewing the order. [https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0685/] [https://www.mexc.com/announcements/article/statement-regarding-fincen-s-november-6-order-17827791539626]
The exchange promises engagement with regulators and an independent examination of its anti-money-laundering and sanctions controls. [https://www.mexc.com/announcements/article/statement-regarding-fincen-s-november-6-order-17827791539626]

TECH:

[TECH] Nethermind's validation defense preserves block processing while transactions wait. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26224]
Nethermind researchers published denial-of-service tests for proposed Ethereum frame transactions on 6 November 2026. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26224]
Their defense pauses transaction validation during block processing, permitting a tested 300,000-gas validation budget against the proposal's 100,000-gas cap. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26224]
Under a flood of 100 failing transactions per second, their slowest machine admitted just 12% of honest frame transactions. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26224]
These are client benchmarks on specified hardware, rather than measurements of a deployed network upgrade. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26224]

[TECH] Walraswap proposes batch settlement using external trading pools. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26219]
Sergio Yuhjtman introduced the research on 5 November 2026, describing equilibrium-based settlement across multiple tokens. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26219]
The proposed calculation combines submitted orders with external automated-market-maker liquidity while leaving a nonnegative surplus of each token. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26219]
External swaps retain their pools' pricing rules; the design relies on governance choosing solvers and public order flow exposing censorship. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26219]
