LEAD:
Treasury orders US banks to stop moving money for MEXC Global.

FinCEN says MEXC Global moved at least $16 billion tied to sanctions evasion and cybercrime between May 2021 and May 2026. Its order bars US financial institutions from any funds transfer to or from the exchange, starting 30 days after Federal Register publication.

On 6 November 2026, the Treasury's Financial Crimes Enforcement Network issued an order cutting MEXC Global off from the US financial system [https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0685]. The action falls under Treasury's campaign named Operation Economic Outcast [https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0685]. The legal basis is section 9714(a) of the Combating Russian Money Laundering Act, not an OFAC sanctions designation [https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0685].

The scale matters. Treasury describes MEXC as a top-20 exchange that processed more than $1 billion a day as of September 2026 and held about $5 billion in reserves [https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0685]. Against that, the alleged $16 billion over five years is roughly sixteen days of current volume.

Treasury breaks the figure down. It cites nearly $1.5 billion linked to Russian sanctions evasion, including $232 million in direct transactions with sanctioned entities [https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0685]. It says MEXC still dealt with Garantex and its successor Grinex as recently as June 2026, more than four years after OFAC designated Garantex in April 2022 [https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0685]. It also cites about $1 billion with more than 40 Iranian exchangers and about $22 million benefiting Iran's central bank and the IRGC [https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0685].

These are Treasury's findings, not a court judgment. "Financial institutions cannot have it both ways," Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said [https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0685]. The Defiant reports that MEXC says withdrawals continue and that it has begun an independent review of its AML and sanctions controls [https://thedefiant.io/news/cefi/mexc-says-withdrawals-continue-as-fincen-orders-us-financial-cutoff]. This edition did not locate a primary MEXC statement. The Federal Register publication date, and so the effective date, was not confirmed at press time.

NEWS:
[NEWS] Gnosis Pay confirms a breach of its legacy consumer-card system.
Gnosis Pay says it detected unauthorized access on 3 November 2026 and began containment within two hours [https://gnosispay.com/blog/data-breach-legacy-system-gnosis-pay-branded-cards]. The company says customer funds were not affected and that its white-label enterprise platform was untouched, according to Decrypt's account of the statement [https://decrypt.co/382718/crypto-card-provider-gnosis-pay-hackers-customer-data]. It has made 3D Secure mandatory for online payments until the legacy system retires on 20 December 2026 [https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2026-11-06-gnosis-pay-data-breach-legacy-debit-card-system-435934]. The company has not disclosed how many customers were affected [https://decrypt.co/382718/crypto-card-provider-gnosis-pay-hackers-customer-data].

[NEWS] Treasury sanctions 45 more targets tied to Prince Group.
Treasury designated four individuals and 41 entities it says helped Prince Group executives hide assets after earlier sanctions in October 2025 and June 2026, Decrypt reports [https://decrypt.co/382730/us-sanctions-45-more-targets-tied-to-alleged-crypto-scam-empire-prince-group]. The primary release is Treasury's sb0683 [https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0683] [https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions/20261105]. Treasury accuses the Cambodian conglomerate of running scam compounds staffed by trafficked workers; Prince Group denies the allegations, according to Decrypt [https://decrypt.co/382730/us-sanctions-45-more-targets-tied-to-alleged-crypto-scam-empire-prince-group].

TECH:
[TECH] Zcash activates NU7 and strands the Sprout pool.
Zcash's NU7 upgrade activated on 5 November 2026 at block height 3,543,405, The Block reports [https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2026-11-06-zcashs-nu7-goes-live-435807]. It cuts the target block interval from 75 to 25 seconds and divides per-block subsidies by three, keeping issuance and the 21 million cap intact [https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2026-11-06-zcashs-nu7-goes-live-435807]. A new mechanism withholds 60% of fees for future block rewards, per Decrypt [https://decrypt.co/382692/zcashs-nu7-upgrade-goes-live-recycling-60-of-fees-to-fund-future-block-rewards]. Because NU7 disallows version 4 transactions, about 12,224 ZEC left in the original Sprout pool is now unspendable [https://cointelegraph.com/news/zcash-nu7-upgrade-15m-sprout-funds-unspendable].

[TECH] A Bitcoin Core developer proposes quantum landmines.
Cory Fields posted "Quantum Landmines and Vulnerable Coin Auctions" to the bitcoin-dev list on 4 November 2026 [https://groups.google.com/g/bitcoindev/c/ARMwWdWcz9A]. Under his scheme, spending a quantum-vulnerable coin after a quantum break would require equal quantum-safe collateral, locked for a window such as 30 days [https://groups.google.com/g/bitcoindev/c/ARMwWdWcz9A]. An owner who planted a hidden proof of key ownership could reclaim the coins and seize the thief's collateral [https://groups.google.com/g/bitcoindev/c/ARMwWdWcz9A]. Fields calls the ideas thought experiments, and no enforcement rules exist [https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/11/05/bitcoin-developer-proposes-landmines-to-make-quantum-thieves-lose-their-own-btc].

[CULTURE] A user's account turns the Gnosis Pay breach into a public dispute.
The Defiant reports that Rotki's founder says the Gnosis Pay breach exposed his address, card and wallet details [https://thedefiant.io/news/security/rotki-founder-says-gnosis-pay-exposed-his-address-card-and-wallet-details]. That account goes further than the company's statement that funds were not affected and its warning to users against sharing their address or holdings [https://decrypt.co/382718/crypto-card-provider-gnosis-pay-hackers-customer-data].
