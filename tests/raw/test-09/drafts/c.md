LEAD:
Chainalysis begins restoring compliance tools after exposing users' credentials. [https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/security-incident-october-30/]

The company's 6 November 2026 update announces a phased reopening of transaction monitoring and address screening. Its investigation tools remain under repair. [https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/security-incident-october-30/]

On 30 October 2026, Chainalysis said it had found no evidence of compromised credentials or session tokens. Its 4 November 2026 disclosure reversed that assessment. Names, usernames, password hashes and live tokens for all users had been exposed, the company said. [https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/security-incident-october-30/]

The affected monitoring service sits inside customers' compliance operations. Chainalysis describes KYT as assessing incoming and outgoing cryptocurrency transfers, tracing sources and destinations associated with illicit activity. Its advertised purpose includes helping customers decide when to freeze funds and integrating transaction checks into their compliance programs. [https://www.chainalysis.com/product/kyt/]

The product also monitors exposure to sanctioned entities and lets teams investigate alerts through Reactor. Chainalysis describes that connection as a route from transaction screening into more detailed investigations. [https://www.chainalysis.com/product/kyt/]

Chainalysis says abnormal activity prompted it to shut down KYT, address screening and the login page on 4 November 2026. It notified customers whose KYT accounts appeared to have suffered unauthorized access. The company said it had no evidence of accessed investigation data or manipulated attribution data. [https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/security-incident-october-30/]

Restored access requires trusted IP addresses, password resets and replacement API keys where applicable. The 6 November 2026 notice gives no completion date for restoring Reactor and Data Solutions. Its reopening announcement therefore establishes partial recovery, not a clean bill of health for every product. [https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/security-incident-october-30/]
