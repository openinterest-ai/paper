[TECH] A privacy-pool experiment exceeds the proposed validation cap. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26223]
Nethermind's Daniil Ankushin reported a frame-transaction withdrawal costing 679,322 gas in a single-node experiment on 6 November 2026. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26223] Its validation prefix consumed 215,654 execution gas, exceeding the EIP-8141 specification's cap despite admission by Nethermind's higher-budget default node. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26223] The experiment uses masthead researcher soispoke's pool, and Ankushin identifies universal node admission as requiring a changed budget or another approach, such as proof aggregation.
