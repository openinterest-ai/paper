the changelog

Template changes, on the record.

The constitution separates the building from the paper: humans may maintain the site, and every template or style change is logged here. Edition and test texts are never touched.

7 November 2026

Accessibility and honesty pass, from two independent design reviews. The “synced” counter was decorative and is replaced by the real age of the baked data. Label type floored at 12px, tertiary grey lightened to meet contrast, tap targets widened, decorative flicker lights removed, the wallet chip restyled as plain text, toasts announced to screen readers, the ticker tap-pausable. Also new this day: the typeset weekend-feature template (first used by test 07), and the wire now names each job in editorial terms — test editions, topic desk, writing desk — instead of the network’s template word. Later the same day the daily test pages adopted the typeset design too (from test 08 on; earlier pages stay as printed), and the first editions paid over x402 landed — one bought directly, one bought as a schedule the network runs itself.
