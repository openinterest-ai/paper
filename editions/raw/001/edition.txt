LEAD:
U.S. authorities obtain court orders over $1.2 billion stolen from Binance.

The Justice Department announced seizure or restraint authority on 12 November 2026. The money forms part of more than $2.7 billion already frozen through public and private efforts. [https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-announces-actions-disrupt-north-koreas-massive-theft-cryptocurrency]

The orders put legal authority behind part of the Binance recovery operation. They cover assets within the freeze total, so adding the two figures would count the same money twice. The department says tracing, freezing, seizure and forfeiture efforts continue. [https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-announces-actions-disrupt-north-koreas-massive-theft-cryptocurrency]

The FBI attributes the theft of more than $6 billion on or about 29 October 2026 to North Korean activity it calls TraderTraitor. Its 31 October 2026 alert said investigators had observed stolen assets being converted primarily into ether. Further movement through decentralized finance and instant swaps was its expectation, rather than a completed finding. [https://www.fbi.gov/investigate/cyber/alerts/2026/north-korea-responsible-for-6-billion-binance-attack]

That alert asks infrastructure operators and exchanges to block transactions involving listed addresses or funds derived from them. It publishes the addresses investigators associate with the theft, including Ethereum address 0x8532FA14ACAf483729C449b8458aedDb900543D4. These are the FBI's attributions, not independently established identities. [https://www.fbi.gov/investigate/cyber/alerts/2026/north-korea-responsible-for-6-billion-binance-attack]

The 12 November 2026 announcement also covers a separate operation against FaceStealer infrastructure. The FBI describes that campaign as using recorded webcam footage and stolen communication accounts to impersonate trusted contacts. It says developers' repository accounts are targets, extending the threat into software distribution. [https://www.fbi.gov/investigate/cyber/facestealer-cryptocurrency-theft-scheme]

Neither operation establishes a payout date for Binance's recovered assets. Associated Press explains that forfeiture proceedings typically require a judge to determine legitimate claims to recovered funds. Court authority over stolen cryptocurrency is therefore a different milestone from returning it. [https://apnews.com/article/41be4834083e596a8facfd719f851cae] [https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-announces-actions-disrupt-north-koreas-massive-theft-cryptocurrency]

[NEWS] Attackers drain Kava Lend through a dormant stablecoin market.
Bitquery's 14 November 2026 investigation reports 55.2 million KAVA borrowed through 127 loans, with 53.9 million retained after repayments. [https://bitquery.io/investigations/kava-hack]
It values the retained coins at about $3.7 million before their sale, distinguishing that amount from roughly $2.1 million in sale proceeds. [https://bitquery.io/investigations/kava-hack]

Bitquery identifies kava1uzx5924cqsutcw365ajs8kkc3rr4vtlyzms255 as the main wallet and reports that liquidations repeatedly erased the helper wallet's debt. [https://bitquery.io/investigations/kava-hack]
The Block's Zack Abrams reports that Kava described the incident as contained within Kava Lend and paused its Axelar bridge as a precaution. [https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2026-11-14-kava-lend-loses-roughly-3-8-million-to-hack-sending-kava-token-down-nearly-40-440350]

[NEWS] The CFTC sues Louisiana over its prediction-market shutdown.
The CFTC announced its federal lawsuit on 13 November 2026, seeking to stop Louisiana from prohibiting event contracts offered through federally regulated firms. [https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9326-26]
Its complaint says a 12 November 2026 state order barred event contracts without a state license, covering sports, elections and other events. [https://www.cftc.gov/media/14936/OGC_LouisianaCompliant111326/download]
The commission argues that federal derivatives law gives it exclusive jurisdiction; that is its litigation position, rather than a ruling in this case. [https://www.cftc.gov/media/14936/OGC_LouisianaCompliant111326/download]

[NEWS] Trezor cuts its reseller network after finding hardware implants.
Trezor said on 12 November 2026 that it had found implants in three Safe 5 devices bought from CryptoBilis. [https://trezor.io/blog/news/our-cryptobilis-investigation-what-we-found-and-what-weve-changed]
The company says it removed about 80% of its reseller network, while describing the removed partners as trustworthy and posing no immediate threat. [https://trezor.io/blog/news/our-cryptobilis-investigation-what-we-found-and-what-weve-changed]

It says firmware released on 11 November 2026 blocks the discovered implants from capturing screen data, but cannot protect recovery words captured beforehand. [https://trezor.io/blog/news/our-cryptobilis-investigation-what-we-found-and-what-weve-changed]
None of the three owners had reported losing funds, according to Trezor. [https://trezor.io/blog/news/our-cryptobilis-investigation-what-we-found-and-what-weve-changed]

[TECH] A TEMBRI correction reverses one historical swap comparison.
Dan Guo disclosed two column-order bugs on 14 November 2026, affecting measurements for 1 July 2025. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26201/3]
His corrected share of multi-pool swaps paying zero tips in the first 5% of block gas is 28%, against 21% on 1 July 2026. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26201/3]
Guo says the correction reverses that comparison while leaving the 2026 figures unchanged; he credits akatsukiwork0320 with finding both bugs. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26201/3]

[TECH] Ethereum fee simulations favor separate prices for separate resources.
Fei Wu's 12 November 2026 research compares proposed fee mechanisms under a calibrated demand model. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26244]
Wu reports that conservative EIP-7999 configurations deliver 4% to 34% more execution than the normalized EIP-8372 benchmark at similarly low congestion. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26244]
The work tests specific designs and demand assumptions, including execution targets beyond observed demand; it establishes simulation results, rather than mainnet performance. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26244]

CULTURE:
[CULTURE] Molly White challenges crypto's election victory scorecard.
In a 13 November 2026 essay, White argues that Fairshake's headline win rate conceals its dependence on safe seats. [https://www.citationneeded.news/crypto-ai-lost-the-midterms/]
Her tally gives the network 74 wins from 74 clear or likely favorites, against 10 from 18 competitive races. [https://www.citationneeded.news/crypto-ai-lost-the-midterms/]
White also records Fairshake's contrary claim that it played a meaningful role in competitive victories, preserving the disagreement over what its spending achieved. [https://www.citationneeded.news/crypto-ai-lost-the-midterms/]

[CULTURE] Cartesi declines to fund a replacement for expiring staking rewards.
The Cartesi Foundation told its governance forum on 13 November 2026 that it does not plan treasury funding to extend Mine Reserve rewards. [https://governance.cartesi.io/t/523/12]
It said no replacement incentive program is planned to coincide with the reserve's projected depletion around July 2027. [https://governance.cartesi.io/t/523/13]

The Foundation also rejected CTSI buybacks and revenue sharing, saying its Knoss agreement funds development rather than distributions to token holders. [https://governance.cartesi.io/t/523/13]
Its separate proposal to remove token-minting authority remains conditional on approval and execution. [https://governance.cartesi.io/t/523]
