OPEN INTEREST / Daily brief, format v2. Sunday, 22 November 2026.

LEAD: Chainflip loses bitcoin and ether in a two-chain vault theft.

Chainflip Labs says the 21 November 2026 theft took about 123 bitcoin as well as ether and dollar tokens. [https://chainflip.io/return] The cross-chain swap service remains unavailable as the company offers to let the attacker keep a share if they return the rest. [https://chainflip.io/] [https://chainflip.io/return]

At 07:01 UTC on 21 November 2026, a Bitcoin transaction sent 122.86 BTC to a single address. [https://mempool.space/tx/ac38a5aea1ed81b84307a3709f502abd4c4d3af5502493f883c6d19624fd2cb4] At 07:10 UTC on 21 November 2026, one Ethereum transaction sent 520.22 ether, 3.25 million USDC and 758,141 USDT from Chainflip’s Ethereum vault to a different address. [https://eth.blockscout.com/tx/0xc928950f79b4e4697af674310d1f3a08c2659adc4d60e719f3af90ab023ff20f] Bitquery valued that Ethereum outflow at $4.86 million, of which USDC accounted for about two-thirds. [https://bitquery.io/investigations/chainflip-hack]

Chainflip holds funds in vaults so users can swap assets across blockchains. [https://bitquery.io/investigations/chainflip-hack] Its validators normally sign Ethereum payouts together, and Bitquery found that the vault accepted the signature on the drain. [https://bitquery.io/investigations/chainflip-hack] The payment fell outside Chainflip’s usual numbering sequence, the only such exception among nearly 300,000 signatures Bitquery examined. [https://bitquery.io/investigations/chainflip-hack]

Bitquery recorded a change of signing key at 08:53 UTC on 21 November 2026. [https://bitquery.io/investigations/chainflip-hack] Chainflip Labs says it identified and addressed the way the key was exposed. [https://chainflip.io/return] Its public notice gives no technical account of how that happened. [https://chainflip.io/return]

The Bitcoin address had recorded no outgoing transfers by 10:30 UTC on 22 November 2026. [https://mempool.space/api/address/bc1qm777twlzmelyutungyfw65ykn2l5kn68e6v4g6] A message confirmed on 21 November 2026 pointed the recipient to Chainflip Labs’ offer. [https://mempool.space/tx/0f75dbd50f247d4d29ffb5d19dae9907f0322f9ef504720b7e27d2453624f004] The company says the recipient can keep 10% of the proceeds if roughly 110.58 BTC and 2,618.1 ETH arrive by 18:38 UTC on 23 November 2026. [https://chainflip.io/return]

Chainflip Labs offered a plan to reimburse affected liquidity providers after a separate loss of about 736,000 USDT from its Tron vault on 12 September 2026. [https://chainflip.io/blog/getting-chainflip-back-up-and-running] The November recovery offer gives no terms for compensating users. [https://chainflip.io/return]

NEWS:

[NEWS] Hong Kong proposes licences for four more crypto services.
The government published a bill on 20 November 2026 covering businesses that deal in, advise on, manage or hold virtual assets for clients. [https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202611/20/P2026112000582p.htm] Providers would need a licence or registration with the Securities and Futures Commission. [https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202611/20/P2026112000582p.htm] Banks offering those services would also need commission registration. [https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202611/20/P2026112000582p.htm] The government scheduled the first legislative reading for 2 December 2026. [https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202611/20/P2026112000582p.htm]

[NEWS] Russia’s rules for organised crypto trading take effect.
The Bank of Russia’s exchange ordinance takes effect on 22 November 2026, after a ten-day interval set by the text. [https://www.cbr.ru/Queries/XsltBlock/File/87500/-1/2630#page=15] It adds reporting requirements for crypto contracts, including trade counts and total volume. [https://www.cbr.ru/Queries/XsltBlock/File/87500/-1/2630#page=13] Under a separate ordinance, the central bank lists bitcoin, ether and USDT as available to non-qualified investors. [https://www.cbr.ru/eng/press/event/?id=32966] The central bank says those investors face a 300,000-ruble annual purchase limit per intermediary. [https://www.cbr.ru/eng/press/event/?id=32966]

[NEWS] Coins.ph ends transfers involving self-custody wallets.
In a notice updated on 21 November 2026, Coins.ph said it accepts external transfers only to and from accounts at regulated exchanges. [https://support.coins.ph/hc/en-us/articles/4403931971225-How-does-the-Travel-Rule-affect-crypto-transfers] The policy took effect on 19 November 2026, and the exchange says it adopted it to comply with Philippine transfer reporting rules. [https://support.coins.ph/hc/en-us/articles/4403931971225-How-does-the-Travel-Rule-affect-crypto-transfers] The exchange asks for the counterparty’s details on every external transfer, regardless of size. [https://support.coins.ph/hc/en-us/articles/4403931971225-How-does-the-Travel-Rule-affect-crypto-transfers]

[TECH] Solidity fixes a compiler bug that can corrupt stored data.
The Solidity team released version 0.8.39 on 19 November 2026. [https://www.soliditylang.org/blog/2026/11/19/solidity-0.8.39-release-announcement/] The fix adds a runtime check for some copies of nested data, increasing their execution cost. [https://www.soliditylang.org/blog/2026/11/19/solidity-0.8.39-release-announcement/] The team says it found no deployed contract performing an affected copy in the Sourcify database of verified contracts. [https://www.soliditylang.org/blog/2026/11/19/solidity-0.8.39-release-announcement/]
