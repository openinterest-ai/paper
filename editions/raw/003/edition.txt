LEAD
The Supreme Court declines Custodia’s challenge to Fed account discretion.

The Supreme Court denied Custodia Bank’s petition on 16 November 2026. [https://www.supremecourt.gov/orders/courtorders/111626zor_cb7d.pdf] The refusal leaves standing a ruling that regional Federal Reserve Banks can reject eligible applicants for master accounts. [https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/26/26-62/416173/20260710114808303_Custodia%20Pet.%20App.%20FINAL.pdf]

Custodia sought direct entry to the Federal Reserve’s payment services through an account at the Kansas City Fed. A master account holds a bank’s deposits at a regional Reserve Bank and provides access to those services. The appeals court records that Kansas City accepted Custodia’s legal eligibility but rejected its crypto-focused business model as unduly risky. [https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/26/26-62/416173/20260710114808303_Custodia%20Pet.%20App.%20FINAL.pdf]

Custodia’s petition argued that the Monetary Control Act requires access for eligible institutions outside the Fed’s membership. It challenged regional Reserve Banks’ authority to refuse them. The bank framed the dispute as a question of statutory entitlement, rather than a request for special treatment. [https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/26/26-62/416173/20260710114750570_Custodia%20cert%20petition%20FINAL_REVISED.pdf]

The Board’s opposition argued that eligibility does not compel approval. It said mandatory access would impair Reserve Banks’ ability to protect the financial system from risky applicants. Its brief also emphasized that Custodia had not challenged the particular reasons for rejecting its application. [https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/26/26-62/429415/20261013190852190_26-62CustodiaBankOpp.pdf]

The Tenth Circuit upheld that discretion on 31 October 2025, over a dissent. Its majority read the statute’s service-access provision as a pricing rule, rather than an automatic right to an account. [https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/26/26-62/416173/20260710114808303_Custodia%20Pet.%20App.%20FINAL.pdf]

The Supreme Court’s order places Custodia under denials of review and supplies no explanation for its refusal. It contains no new merits opinion resolving the competing statutory arguments. The decision it leaves in place separates permission to apply from entitlement to receive an account. [https://www.supremecourt.gov/orders/courtorders/111626zor_cb7d.pdf] [https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/26/26-62/416173/20260710114808303_Custodia%20Pet.%20App.%20FINAL.pdf]

[NEWS] Lighter reopens withdrawals after a trading-key incident.

Lighter announced on 16 November 2026 that deposits and withdrawals had returned to normal after its Security Council disabled Guardian Mode. [https://t.me/s/lighter_announcements/484] CoinDesk reports that the exchange froze withdrawals after a trader’s API key leaked; Lighter said its protocol and smart contracts were unaffected. [https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/11/16/lighter-suspends-all-withdrawals-after-usd10-4-million-crypto-exploit] The company’s reopening notice confirms its reported service status, but gives no final loss figure. [https://t.me/s/lighter_announcements/484]

[NEWS] A former Robinhood engineer pleads guilty over Hyperliquid trades.

Huaisong Xiang pleaded guilty to wire fraud on 16 November 2026, the U.S. Attorney’s Office for the Southern District of New York announced. [https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/former-robinhood-employee-pleads-guilty-fraud] Prosecutors say he used confidential Robinhood listing information to trade perpetual futures on Hyperliquid before public announcements. [https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/former-robinhood-employee-pleads-guilty-fraud] He agreed to forfeit $125,523, the proceeds prosecutors attribute to the scheme, with sentencing scheduled for 25 February 2027. [https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/former-robinhood-employee-pleads-guilty-fraud]

[NEWS] Luxor promises reimbursement after miners’ work is redirected.

CoinDesk reported on 16 November 2026 that attackers used Luxor’s Commander management software to redirect customers’ machines to another mining pool. [https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/11/16/bitcoin-miners-were-hijacked-and-their-btc-rewards-redirected-to-an-attacker] According to its account of Luxor’s disclosure, just over a dozen customer accounts were affected, and engineers patched Commander within two hours. [https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/11/16/bitcoin-miners-were-hijacked-and-their-btc-rewards-redirected-to-an-attacker] Luxor promised reimbursement; CoinDesk’s report does not establish that customers have received it. [https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/11/16/bitcoin-miners-were-hijacked-and-their-btc-rewards-redirected-to-an-attacker]

TECH
[TECH] Signature experiments expose unfinished Ethereum integration work.

Manuel Santos published experiments on 16 November 2026 proving verification of five post-quantum signature schemes through leanVM. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26261] His implementation fits four schemes’ individual verifications into one STARK proof; Raccoon-G requires splitting the work and combining proofs. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26261] 

Santos proposes moving expensive proving to outside aggregators while wallets retain their signing keys. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26261] He identifies unfinished generic-proof rules in draft EIP-8288, so the measurements establish experimental performance rather than a deployed wallet feature. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26261]

[TECH] A data correction reverses one finding about zero-tip swaps.

Masthead researcher DanGuo disclosed two TEMBRI column-reading bugs on 14 November 2026, affecting an older data layout for 1 July 2025. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26201/3] The corrected share of multi-pool swaps paying zero tips in the first 5% of block gas rose from 10.4% to 28.0%. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26201/3] That reverses the comparison with 21.0% on 1 July 2026; DanGuo says the 2026 figures and broader conclusions remain unchanged. [https://ethresear.ch/t/26201/3]

[CULTURE] Cardano voters reject a treasury-funded copper reserve.

CoinDesk reported on 16 November 2026 that Toto Finance’s request for 22 million ADA expired without approval on 15 November 2026. [https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/16/cardano-holders-vote-down-usd5-million-bid-to-buy-warehouse-copper] The governance record shows 2.86 billion ADA in explicit opposition against 20.02 million ADA supporting the request, a ratio exceeding 140 to one. [https://www.cardanocube.com/governance/gov_actions/gov_action1dmwlfuwm45r0x2d2d96r2kwyjv9hk6ylzmq8xpsay2xecesn6xwqqdlv55z] 

Toto proposed putting roughly two-thirds of the withdrawal into physical copper and returning net sale proceeds to the treasury. [https://www.cardanocube.com/governance/gov_actions/gov_action1dmwlfuwm45r0x2d2d96r2kwyjv9hk6ylzmq8xpsay2xecesn6xwqqdlv55z] Its proposal explicitly guaranteed neither recovery of the original ADA nor profit. [https://www.cardanocube.com/governance/gov_actions/gov_action1dmwlfuwm45r0x2d2d96r2kwyjv9hk6ylzmq8xpsay2xecesn6xwqqdlv55z]

[CULTURE] Molly White disputes Fairshake’s election victory accounting.

In her 13 November 2026 analysis, masthead writer Molly White challenges Fairshake’s practice of counting races without distinguishing their competitiveness. [https://www.citationneeded.news/crypto-ai-lost-the-midterms/] She reports that its network won all 74 races rated safe or likely, compared with ten of eighteen competitive races. [https://www.citationneeded.news/crypto-ai-lost-the-midterms/] White argues that the weaker competitive results diminish the industry’s political intimidation power; that is her interpretation, not a measured change in congressional behavior. [https://www.citationneeded.news/crypto-ai-lost-the-midterms/]
