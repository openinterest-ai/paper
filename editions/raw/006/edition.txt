LEAD
An Abu Dhabi court grants Binance an order to preserve stolen crypto.

Binance disclosed the order on 18 November 2026 as it sought to recover assets from the 29 October 2026 theft. [https://www.binance.com/en/blog/community/4100322350300314528] Reuters reports that a court order requires 18 companies to keep specified tokens frozen. [https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/UAE%2Bcourt%2Borders%2Bfreeze%2Bon%2Bcrypto%2Btokens%2Bstolen%2Bfrom%2BBinance%2Bwallets/27369327.html]

A token can be frozen and still be out of reach of the person claiming it. [https://www.binance.com/en/blog/community/4100322350300314528][https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/UAE%2Bcourt%2Borders%2Bfreeze%2Bon%2Bcrypto%2Btokens%2Bstolen%2Bfrom%2BBinance%2Bwallets/27369327.html] Binance says some issuers, exchanges and other intermediaries require a court order before they will release or return assets they have already blocked. [https://www.binance.com/en/blog/community/4100322350300314528] The exchange says it has absorbed the financial impact for its users and is pursuing the assets separately. [https://www.binance.com/en/blog/community/4100322350300314528]

The Block reports that the worldwide injunction against unidentified alleged attackers covers up to $6.5 billion. [https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-18-binance-freezing-court-order-adgm-crypto-october-hack-443491] That is the order’s ceiling, against roughly $6.34 billion in assets affected by the theft, according to figures reported by The Block. [https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-18-binance-freezing-court-order-adgm-crypto-october-hack-443491] The same report cites a prior Binance estimate of about $2.85 billion frozen or intercepted. [https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-11-18-binance-freezing-court-order-adgm-crypto-october-hack-443491]

Reuters says the separate order applies to USDC issuer Circle, USD1 issuer World Liberty Financial and 16 other companies. [https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/UAE%2Bcourt%2Borders%2Bfreeze%2Bon%2Bcrypto%2Btokens%2Bstolen%2Bfrom%2BBinance%2Bwallets/27369327.html] It bars them from lending, swapping or mixing the affected tokens, and the court alleges no wrongdoing by those companies, Reuters reports. [https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/UAE%2Bcourt%2Borders%2Bfreeze%2Bon%2Bcrypto%2Btokens%2Bstolen%2Bfrom%2BBinance%2Bwallets/27369327.html] Tether falls outside that order and told Reuters it would work directly with Binance on reissuing tokens, in consultation with the authorities. [https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/UAE%2Bcourt%2Borders%2Bfreeze%2Bon%2Bcrypto%2Btokens%2Bstolen%2Bfrom%2BBinance%2Bwallets/27369327.html]

In the United States, the Justice Department said on 12 November 2026 it had obtained authority to seize or restrain over $1.2 billion. [https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-announces-actions-disrupt-north-koreas-massive-theft-cryptocurrency] The department included that sum in the more than $2.7 billion it said government and private-sector efforts had already frozen. [https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-announces-actions-disrupt-north-koreas-massive-theft-cryptocurrency]

Reuters reports that the Abu Dhabi court will consider on 1 December 2026 whether its order preserving the specified assets should continue. [https://www.streetinsider.com/Reuters/UAE%2Bcourt%2Borders%2Bfreeze%2Bon%2Bcrypto%2Btokens%2Bstolen%2Bfrom%2BBinance%2Bwallets/27369327.html] Binance’s announcement gives no total returned under the new orders. [https://www.binance.com/en/blog/community/4100322350300314528]

[NEWS] The SEC sues eight entities over alleged sham crypto trading.
The SEC announced the Colorado federal case on 19 November 2026 and alleges U.S. investors lost more than $37 million. [https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-115-sec-eight-entities-used-fake-crypto-asset-trading-platforms-steal-more-37-million-us-investors] It says four pairs of purported advisers and trading platforms solicited money from August 2024 through May 2026. [https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-115-sec-eight-entities-used-fake-crypto-asset-trading-platforms-steal-more-37-million-us-investors] The agency alleges the operators used both fake certificates and actual regulatory filings to make the businesses appear legitimate. [https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-115-sec-eight-entities-used-fake-crypto-asset-trading-platforms-steal-more-37-million-us-investors][https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/complaints/2026/comp-pr2026-115.pdf]

[NEWS] MoonPay applies for a U.S. national trust charter.
The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency lists the application as received on 18 November 2026. [https://www.occ.gov/topics/charters-and-licensing/digital-assets-licensing-applications/index-digital-assets-licensing-applications.html] MoonPay proposes a non-deposit-taking bank that would hold assets in custody and issue and redeem stablecoins. [https://occ.gov/topics/charters-and-licensing/digital-assets-licensing-applications/moonpay-national-trust-bank-combined-cv-pa.pdf] The filing also proposes managing stablecoin reserves and handling conversions as an agent. [https://occ.gov/topics/charters-and-licensing/digital-assets-licensing-applications/moonpay-national-trust-bank-combined-cv-pa.pdf]

[NEWS] HSBC launches tokenised shares in its Hong Kong dollar money market fund.
HSBC dates the new share class’s public launch to 18 November 2026, more than 35 years after the fund opened. [https://www.assetmanagement.hsbc.com.hk/-/media/Files/attachments/hongkong/hsbc-global-money-funds-hong-kong-dollar/hsbc-gmf-tokenised-share-class-brochure-hk-retail.pdf] Investors can subscribe and redeem through eligible distributors. [https://www.assetmanagement.hsbc.com.hk/-/media/Files/attachments/hongkong/hsbc-global-money-funds-hong-kong-dollar/hsbc-gmf-tokenised-share-class-brochure-hk-retail.pdf] The class reinvests dividends in additional tokenised units instead of paying them out in cash. [https://www.assetmanagement.hsbc.com.hk/-/media/Files/attachments/hongkong/hsbc-global-money-funds-hong-kong-dollar/hsbc-gmf-tokenised-share-class-brochure-hk-retail.pdf]

[TECH] Polygon activates Purley on its main network.
Polygon Labs says the upgrade took effect at block 58,280,000 at 01:39 UTC on 20 November 2026. [https://forum.polygon.technology/t/heimdall-v0-14-0-released-for-mainnet-and-amoy/22597] The consensus software now carries signed validator vote extensions in the blocks that include them. [https://forum.polygon.technology/t/heimdall-v0-14-0-released-for-mainnet-and-amoy/22597] The upgrade also requires confirmation of bridge events before the network acts on them. [https://forum.polygon.technology/t/heimdall-v0-14-0-released-for-mainnet-and-amoy/22597]

[TECH] ProbeLab puts an outside view of Ethereum nodes in beta.
ProbeLab described Sonar on Ethereum Research on 19 November 2026. [https://ethresear.ch/t/probing-ethereum-nodes-using-sonar/26268] Sonar tries to connect to each address a node advertises, giving operators a peer’s view of whether the node answers. [https://ethresear.ch/t/probing-ethereum-nodes-using-sonar/26268] ProbeLab runs the checks from one region, which limits what a failed connection can establish about reachability elsewhere. [https://ethresear.ch/t/probing-ethereum-nodes-using-sonar/26268]
