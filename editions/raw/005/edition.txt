OPEN INTEREST | 19 November 2026

LEAD

U.S. investigators open a center to interrupt scam payments. [https://www.hstf.gov/press/threat-disruption-center/]

The Homeland Security Task Force launched the Threat Disruption Center on 18 November 2026 to counter foreign scam networks targeting Americans. [https://www.hstf.gov/press/threat-disruption-center/] Blockchain investigator TRM Labs will supply the platform that connects investigators with companies that can intervene. [https://www.hstf.gov/press/threat-disruption-center/] [https://www.trmlabs.com/resources/blog/trm-labs-threat-disruption-center]

The new unit gives investigators a standing channel to the companies that handle payments or host scam accounts. The launch notice says investigators can use the center to sort complaints, map criminal networks and contact companies positioned to warn victims or pause payments. The resource serves a task force spanning more than 30 federal agencies and more than 440 state and local partners. [https://www.hstf.gov/press/threat-disruption-center/]

Complaints involving cryptocurrency accounted for $11.4 billion of the $20.9 billion in losses reported to the FBI’s Internet Crime Complaint Center in 2025. The FBI says its existing Operation Level Up warned 3,780 victims of crypto investment fraud in 2025. The FBI says 78% of the people it warned were unaware they were being scammed. [https://www.ic3.gov/AnnualReport/Reports/2025_IC3Report.pdf]

The center implements a 6 March 2026 executive order directing agencies to coordinate their response to foreign cybercrime networks. [https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/03/combating-cybercrime-fraud-and-predatory-schemes-against-american-citizens/] TRM says it will deploy its Ardence investigations platform and assign engineers and investigators to support it. [https://www.trmlabs.com/resources/blog/trm-labs-threat-disruption-center]

The task force says participating companies can act under their own terms of service. That can mean pausing a payment or suspending a scammer’s account while investigators build a case. The notice describes seizures as a separate step requiring court authorization. [https://www.hstf.gov/press/threat-disruption-center/]

Victims still file reports through the FBI’s complaint center, according to the launch notice. The notice gives no target for how quickly a warning will reach a company or how much money the unit expects to recover. [https://www.hstf.gov/press/threat-disruption-center/]

[TECH] Csaba Kiraly proposes sending encrypted Ethereum block data before revealing it. [https://ethresear.ch/t/reveal-a-key-not-a-payload-sending-the-epbs-payload-before-the-reveal/26264]
On 18 November 2026, Kiraly proposes giving nodes more time to receive the transaction data inside an Ethereum block. Under his design, a builder sends encrypted data once a block includes its bid, then publishes the short key that makes the data readable. Kiraly says the same design gives builders longer to decide whether to withhold the key. He leaves the time savings to measurement and says temporary data known as blobs need a separate solution. [https://ethresear.ch/t/reveal-a-key-not-a-payload-sending-the-epbs-payload-before-the-reveal/26264]

[CULTURE] A dYdX proposal seeks a temporary exception for stranded deposits. [https://dydx.forum/t/drc-temporarily-re-enable-noble-deposits-on-dydx-chain/5385]
