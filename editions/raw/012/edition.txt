OPEN INTEREST / Daily brief, format v2. Thursday, 26 November 2026.

LEAD: Ukraine’s parliament passes a bill to license crypto firms and tax gains.

The Verkhovna Rada approved a framework for crypto trading and custody on 25 November 2026. [https://www.rada.gov.ua/en/news/summari_pz/277070.html] Firms would have to keep customer assets separate from their own. [https://www.rada.gov.ua/en/news/summari_pz/277070.html]

The Rada counted 271 votes in favour on the final reading of bill 10225-d. [https://t.me/verkhovnaradaukrainy/89726?embed=1] The committee text sets 1 July 2027 as the start date for most provisions. [https://itd.rada.gov.ua/billinfo/Bills/pubFile/3777229] The parliamentary record lists the bill as being prepared for signature. [https://itd.rada.gov.ua/billinfo/Bills/Card/56271]

Ukraine had already passed a virtual assets law in 2022. [https://bank.gov.ua/admin_uploads/article/Strategy_finsector_zvit_2024_dod_en.pdf?v=13] The National Bank’s report on 2024 recorded that the law still had to take effect. [https://bank.gov.ua/admin_uploads/article/Strategy_finsector_zvit_2024_dod_en.pdf?v=13] Lawmakers approved the new bill in principle on 3 September 2025 and sent it back for revisions. [https://itd.rada.gov.ua/billinfo/Bills/Card/56271]

The securities commission will authorise and supervise firms within its remit. [https://www.nssmc.gov.ua/ua-verkhovna-rada-ukhvalyla-zakonoproiekt-pro-rehuliuvannia-rynku-virtualnykh-aktyviv-v-ukraini/] The central bank will oversee some classes of assets and handle questions of monetary and financial stability. [https://www.nssmc.gov.ua/ua-verkhovna-rada-ukhvalyla-zakonoproiekt-pro-rehuliuvannia-rynku-virtualnykh-aktyviv-v-ukraini/] The securities commission says the bill draws on the EU’s Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation. [https://www.nssmc.gov.ua/ua-verkhovna-rada-ukhvalyla-zakonoproiekt-pro-rehuliuvannia-rynku-virtualnykh-aktyviv-v-ukraini/]

Finance committee chair Danylo Hetmantsev, the bill’s author, said on 26 November 2026 that the tax would apply to profits from crypto transactions. [https://www.rada.gov.ua/news/news_fr/277080.html] The committee text calculates taxable gains on an annual basis and excludes swaps between crypto assets when no cash changes hands. [https://itd.rada.gov.ua/billinfo/Bills/pubFile/3777229] It gives holders of assets acquired before the rules take effect a separate option for sales into cash through 31 December 2028. [https://itd.rada.gov.ua/billinfo/Bills/pubFile/3777229]

The committee’s second-reading text gives firms a route to apply to the securities commission from 1 May 2027. [https://itd.rada.gov.ua/billinfo/Bills/pubFile/3777229] Those authorisations would take effect from 1 July 2027. [https://itd.rada.gov.ua/billinfo/Bills/pubFile/3777229] The commission says its next job is to write the rules firms will need to operate. [https://www.nssmc.gov.ua/ua-verkhovna-rada-ukhvalyla-zakonoproiekt-pro-rehuliuvannia-rynku-virtualnykh-aktyviv-v-ukraini/]

NEWS:

[NEWS] A US bitcoin reserve bill reaches the House calendar.
The House Financial Services Committee formally reported the amended bill on 24 November 2026, placing it on the Union Calendar. [https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr8957rh/html/BILLS-119hr8957rh.htm] It would establish a bitcoin reserve and a separate stockpile for other digital assets. [https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr8957rh/html/BILLS-119hr8957rh.htm] The bill would bar Treasury from selling or pledging reserve bitcoin for at least 20 years from enactment. [https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr8957rh/html/BILLS-119hr8957rh.htm]

[NEWS] Bithumb says a data breach exposed passport numbers and wallet addresses.
On 25 November 2026, the exchange said it had identified more people and more kinds of data affected by the breach it disclosed on 29 October 2026. [https://m-feed.bithumb.com/notice/1655458] Bithumb said the breach of internal office software exposed data including passport numbers, bank account numbers and crypto wallet addresses. [https://m-feed.bithumb.com/notice/1655458] It said it had found no evidence of direct access to its exchange systems or customer database. [https://m-feed.bithumb.com/notice/1655458]

[NEWS] France’s Senate rejects disclosure for large self-custodied wallets.
Senators rejected two identical amendments during the 24 November 2026 sitting on the 2027 budget. [https://www.senat.fr/cra/s20261124/s20261124_2.html] They would have required taxpayers to report crypto they held outside a service provider if it was worth at least €100,000 at the end of the tax year. [https://www.senat.fr/amendements/2026-2027/155/Amdt_I-2447.html] The Senate also rejected a measure that would have taxed gains when taxpayers swapped crypto for stablecoins classified as electronic money under EU rules. [https://www.senat.fr/cra/s20261124/s20261124_2.html] [https://www.senat.fr/amendements/2026-2027/155/Amdt_I-2421.html]

TECH:

[TECH] Anza proposes dropping work on Solana blocks that have already lost.
Anza developer Philippe Laferrière proposed letting Agave validators stop executing a candidate block once the network finalises a rival for the same slot. [https://github.com/anza-xyz/agave/pull/17044] On 24 November 2026 he opened a prerequisite change to let the replay process keep moving while transaction execution finishes. [https://github.com/anza-xyz/agave/pull/17065]

He says the process can still wait for a transaction already in progress when a block is aborted. [https://github.com/anza-xyz/agave/pull/17065] Both changes remain open for review. [https://github.com/anza-xyz/agave/pull/17044] [https://github.com/anza-xyz/agave/pull/17065]

[TECH] Base plans a transaction format with room for new signatures.
In an interview published on 23 November 2026, Base creator Jesse Pollak said Everest would lay groundwork for wallets to adopt quantum-resistant signatures. [https://cointelegraph.com/interview/base-quantum-security-2027-priority-regulators-warn-risks] The new format would let users keep their addresses when adding new signature methods, according to the interview. [https://cointelegraph.com/interview/base-quantum-security-2027-priority-regulators-warn-risks]

The draft format still uses conventional signatures. [https://github.com/ethereum/EIPs/pull/12385] Base lists no launch date for its test network or main network. [https://docs.base.org/upgrades/everest/overview]

CULTURE:

[CULTURE] Uniswap contributors debate who can steer a trade.
On 24 November 2026, Uniswap’s forum put a standard for declaring a token’s preferred or required trading pools into final review. [https://gov.uniswap.org/t/urc-5-token-venue-policy/26307] A Uniswap Labs routing contributor argued that routing software should be free to choose any trade the token’s code actually allows. [https://gov.uniswap.org/t/urc-5-token-venue-policy/26307]

The proposal’s author agreed to make guidance for routers voluntary and reserve the term “enforced” for restrictions the token can impose. [https://gov.uniswap.org/t/urc-5-token-venue-policy/26307] The forum set 7 December 2026 as the deadline for comments. [https://gov.uniswap.org/t/urc-5-token-venue-policy/26307]

[CULTURE] Senators and DeFi advocates split over the SEC’s proposed crypto rules.
The Senate Banking Committee minority released a letter on 25 November 2026 asking the SEC to drop its proposed crypto offering rules. [https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/warren-van-hollen-and-colleagues-call-on-sec-to-rescind-efforts-to-create-special-treatment-for-crypto-assets-and-tokenized-securities] Senator Elizabeth Warren and her co-signers objected to allowing issuers to certify without SEC review that they had stopped managing a project. [https://www.banking.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/112426_letter_to_sec_re_regulation_crypto_assetspdf.pdf]

On 20 October 2026, the DeFi Education Fund’s Amanda Tuminelli and Ayana Dow backed a route out of securities treatment for tokens on decentralised networks. [https://www.sec.gov/comments/S7-2026-27/s7202627-1103240-3882927.pdf] They argued that network governance should be able to appoint someone to file the exit report when the original issuer is absent. [https://www.sec.gov/comments/S7-2026-27/s7202627-1103240-3882927.pdf]
