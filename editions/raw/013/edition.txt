LEAD: Spain moves to let its financial intelligence unit freeze crypto accounts.

A decree published on 25 November 2026 would let Spain’s financial intelligence unit order temporary account freezes before a court weighs in. The power is due to take effect on 25 January 2027, subject to a vote in the Congress of Deputies. [https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-24702] [https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1978-31229#a86]

Spain put the new power in the sixth final provision of a decree on consumer credit and the sale of bad loans. That provision amends the country’s anti-money-laundering law. It lets the financial intelligence unit, known as Sepblac, intervene when it sees indications of money laundering or terrorist financing. [https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-24702]

Sepblac can suspend a crypto, bank or payment account for up to five working days. It can halt a transaction already in progress for up to ten working days. Either suspension must end sooner if the unit no longer needs it to preserve funds or investigate the suspicion. [https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-24702]

The decree’s preamble says the old power was limited to requests from foreign financial intelligence units. The new article lets Sepblac act on its own analysis, on a firm’s suspicious activity report or at the request of Spanish police. For a transaction flagged by a firm, Sepblac has three working days from the report to order a halt. [https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-24702]

A firm that ignores a suspension order can face sanctions under the anti-money-laundering law. The decree shields firms from liability when they comply with a valid order. Spain’s government says the change gives investigators time to catch up with funds that can move before they finish analysing a case. [https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-24702]

The court comes in when the government wants a suspension to last beyond the limit. The public prosecutor must apply before the deadline. Without judicial ratification or extension, the suspension ends. [https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-24702]
