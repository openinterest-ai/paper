LEAD:
A court places Backward Hat Capital under a receiver.
[https://www.cftc.gov/media/14981/ENF_BackwardHatCapitalFundTROOrder111926/download]

The SEC disclosed emergency court relief on 20 November 2026 over an alleged $560 million fraud involving more than 2,000 investors. [https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-116-sec-charges-indianapolis-based-backward-hat-capital-its-co-founders-560-million-dollar-offering] A receiver has authority to take control of the funds, their records and specified defendants' assets. [https://www.cftc.gov/media/14981/ENF_BackwardHatCapitalFundTROOrder111926/download]

The pitch was to convert investors' money into crypto, then use an artificial-intelligence algorithm for foreign-exchange arbitrage, the SEC alleges. [https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/complaints/2026/comp-pr2026-116.pdf] The promised return was at least 6% per month, enough to more than double an investment over a year. [https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/complaints/2026/comp-pr2026-116.pdf] [https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/two-individuals-charged-600-million-ponzi-scheme] The CFTC alleges little or no foreign-exchange trading occurred, while the defendants continued soliciting money through October 2026. [https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9328-26]

Instead, the SEC alleges co-founders Phillip Williams and Devin Johnson directed more than $250 million into payments to earlier investors. [https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/complaints/2026/comp-pr2026-116.pdf] Another $220 million-plus went to the founders or their businesses, according to the complaint. [https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/complaints/2026/comp-pr2026-116.pdf] Together, those uses consumed over 83% of the money raised, the agency alleges. [https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/complaints/2026/comp-pr2026-116.pdf]

The agencies' fundraising estimates differ: the CFTC alleges more than $550 million, the SEC approximately $560 million, and federal prosecutors more than $600 million. [https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9328-26] [https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-116-sec-charges-indianapolis-based-backward-hat-capital-its-co-founders-560-million-dollar-offering] [https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/two-individuals-charged-600-million-ponzi-scheme] These figures describe alleged fundraising, not an inventory of recovered assets. [https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9328-26] [https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/two-individuals-charged-600-million-ponzi-scheme]

An Indiana federal court froze assets on 18 November 2026 and appointed Kevin Duff temporary receiver on 19 November 2026. [https://www.cftc.gov/media/14976/ENF_BackwardHatCapitalFundTROAssetFreezeOrder111826/download] [https://www.cftc.gov/media/14981/ENF_BackwardHatCapitalFundTROOrder111926/download] Duff can remove managers and take possession of assets and records, including control of cryptographic keys. [https://www.cftc.gov/media/14981/ENF_BackwardHatCapitalFundTROOrder111926/download]

The SEC also accuses Joseph Anderson of supplying purportedly independent audit reports that falsely verified the funds' returns. [https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/complaints/2026/comp-pr2026-116.pdf] Prosecutors announced Williams's and Johnson's arrests on 18 November 2026; the criminal charges remain allegations. [https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/two-individuals-charged-600-million-ponzi-scheme] The agency announcements provide no repayment timetable for investors. [https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-116-sec-charges-indianapolis-based-backward-hat-capital-its-co-founders-560-million-dollar-offering] [https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9328-26]

[NEWS] The Senate advances a ban on war and death contracts.
[https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CREC-2026-11-19/pdf/CREC-2026-11-19-dailydigest.pdf]

The Senate passed the DEATH BETS Act on 19 November 2026. [https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CREC-2026-11-19/pdf/CREC-2026-11-19-dailydigest.pdf] It would bar federally registered prediction-market venues from listing or clearing contracts involving war, terrorism, assassination or individual deaths. [https://www.curtis.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/curtis-applauds-senate-passage-of-bipartisan-death-bets-act/] An amendment preserves existing law governing other event contracts, according to co-sponsor John Curtis. [https://www.curtis.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/curtis-applauds-senate-passage-of-bipartisan-death-bets-act/]

The measure still needs House approval, The Block reports. [https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-11-20-senate-death-bets-act-444200]

[TECH] A study measures how long an Ethereum market maker waits to learn its trades.
[https://ethresear.ch/t/anatomy-of-a-fill-propamms-on-ethereum/26271]

POL Finance researchers published a study on 19 November 2026 of proprietary automated market makers, pools priced by individual trading firms. [https://ethresear.ch/t/anatomy-of-a-fill-propamms-on-ethereum/26271] They report that El Zorro's node saw blocks a median 1.23 seconds after quotes were signed, across 404 wrapped-ether trades. [https://ethresear.ch/t/anatomy-of-a-fill-propamms-on-ethereum/26271]

The sample covers 14–15 October 2026 and one market maker. [https://ethresear.ch/t/anatomy-of-a-fill-propamms-on-ethereum/26271] The authors argue that this interval leaves the maker unable to hedge an executed trade it does not yet know about. [https://ethresear.ch/t/anatomy-of-a-fill-propamms-on-ethereum/26271]

[CULTURE] SIPO reports that Cardano's police-evidence funding request failed.
[https://sipo.tokyo/language/en/weekly-brief-33-en/]

Delegate SIPO reports that the 9.6 million ADA request for an Indian police-evidence system expired on 20 November 2026 UTC. [https://sipo.tokyo/language/en/weekly-brief-33-en/] Its tally puts support at 41.59% of the eligible delegated stake, excluding abstentions, against the 67% threshold. [https://sipo.tokyo/language/en/weekly-brief-33-en/] Five committee members deemed it constitutional; that finding did not supply the missing delegate support, SIPO reports. [https://sipo.tokyo/language/en/weekly-brief-33-en/]

SIPO, which voted for the proposal, reports that the requested money stays in the treasury. [https://sipo.tokyo/language/en/weekly-brief-33-en/]

[CULTURE] Ethereum researchers dispute which features quantum security should displace.
[https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/thoughts-on-pqai-hegota-and-focil/30421]

Thomas Thiery argued on 17 November 2026 for retaining FOCIL, a proposed censorship-resistance mechanism, while accelerating quantum-resistant cryptography. [https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/thoughts-on-pqai-hegota-and-focil/30421] He warned that dropping agreed features could damage core developers' credibility. [https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/thoughts-on-pqai-hegota-and-focil/30421] Researcher Nerolation replied on 18 November 2026 that additional features multiply the code and interactions developers must secure. [https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/thoughts-on-pqai-hegota-and-focil/30421/2]

Nerolation favours narrower upgrades, with FOCIL returning after the quantum-resistant consensus design becomes clearer. [https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/thoughts-on-pqai-hegota-and-focil/30421/2]
