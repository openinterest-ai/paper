FinCEN takes systems offline after unauthorised access and extends filing deadlines.

The financial-crime agency says a limited subset of its systems was accessed without authorisation. [1] Its 24 November 2026 order gives specified filers until 30 December 2026 to submit reports while keeping monitoring duties intact. [2]

FinCEN collects, analyses and distributes financial intelligence to counter money laundering and terrorism financing. [3] Guidance dated 9 May 2019 explains how Bank Secrecy Act reporting duties apply to qualifying businesses that transfer convertible virtual currencies. [4]

Its public alert says the agency took some systems offline as a precaution after unauthorised access. [1] The Bank Secrecy Act filing website remains operational, although the notice warns that some services may be unavailable. [1]

The order covers submissions otherwise due between 18 November 2026 and 29 December 2026. [2] Covered filers must submit those records by 30 December 2026 instead. [2] FinCEN says a service disruption is preventing filers from meeting their obligations. [2]

For Bank Secrecy Act reports, eligibility depends on using Secure Data Transfer Mode as of 18 November 2026. [2] The relief also covers companies required to file beneficial ownership reports and individuals required to update a FinCEN identifier. [2]

Other anti-money-laundering and counterterrorism-financing requirements remain in force. [2] Financial institutions must continue monitoring for suspicious transactions. [2] An unaffected hotline remains available for urgent information destined for law enforcement. [2]

The alert does not identify who obtained access or name the affected systems. [1] It does not say whether financial reports were accessed, or explain how the intrusion happened. [1] The agency promises to restore normal operations but gives no restoration date. [1]

South Korea proposes a route for domestic token offerings.

South Korea's Financial Services Commission outlined a digital-assets bill on 24 November 2026. [5] It would permit domestic token offerings after white-paper disclosures and initially restrict stablecoin issuance to consortiums with banks owning more than half the equity. [5] Providers would face strict liability for hacks and technology failures, with penalties up to 10% of sales for breaching safety duties. [5]

CFTC staff accept some crypto assets toward operating reserves.

On 23 November 2026, CFTC staff said bitcoin, ether and qualifying payment stablecoins could count toward derivatives venues' annual operating-cost reserves. [6] The valuation discounts are at least 20% for bitcoin and ether, and 2% for payment stablecoins. [7]

These assets remain ineligible for separate liquidity requirements. [6] The advisory expresses staff views and creates no new Commission rule. [6]

SBI and its partners start their Solana venture.

SBI Holdings, Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group and the Solana Foundation announced completed agreements for SBI Solana Global on 25 November 2026. [8] The renamed SBI R3 Japan plans stablecoin payments, tokenised financial and physical assets, and blockchain asset management. [8] The partners say they will establish use cases in Japan before extending the business across Asia and other markets. [8]

Cancelled bids can leave Ethereum's block builders carrying the risk.

Ethereum validators can select an earlier bid after its builder lowers the offer, Bruno Mazorra and boz1 explained on 24 November 2026. [9] Their model finds that ignoring cancellations shifts risk onto builders without changing expected proposer revenue, provided proposers commit to their selection rule. [9]

Their data covers 1 August to 15 November 2026; holding observed bids fixed, 219 of 584,289 slots with repeated requests produced half the calculated gain from ignoring cancellations. [9] The authors detect the practice in aggregate but cannot identify individual proposers using it. [9]

tech

Felföldi explores verifiable transaction searches without a protocol upgrade.

On 22 November 2026, Zsolt Felföldi estimated that his proposed transaction-and-log index adds 1–2% to total block-processing time. [10] He plans an optional Geth implementation that nodes could run without changing Ethereum's consensus rules. [10] Lightweight clients would need proof that an index is authentic; he is exploring zero-knowledge proofs to provide that without a network upgrade. [10]
