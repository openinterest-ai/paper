A court places Backward Hat Capital under a receiver.

The SEC disclosed emergency court relief on 20 November 2026 over an alleged $560 million fraud involving more than 2,000 investors. [1] A receiver has authority to take control of the funds, their records and specified defendants' assets. [2]

The pitch was to convert investors' money into crypto, then use an artificial-intelligence algorithm for foreign-exchange arbitrage, the SEC alleges. [3] The promised return was at least 6% per month, enough to more than double an investment over a year. [3] [4] The CFTC alleges little or no foreign-exchange trading occurred, while the defendants continued soliciting money through October 2026. [5]

Instead, the SEC alleges co-founders Phillip Williams and Devin Johnson directed more than $250 million into payments to earlier investors. [3] Another $220 million-plus went to the founders or their businesses, according to the complaint. [3] Together, those uses consumed over 83% of the money raised, the agency alleges. [3]

The agencies' fundraising estimates differ: the CFTC alleges more than $550 million, the SEC approximately $560 million, and federal prosecutors more than $600 million. [5] [1] [4] These figures describe alleged fundraising, not an inventory of recovered assets. [5] [4]

An Indiana federal court froze assets on 18 November 2026 and appointed Kevin Duff temporary receiver on 19 November 2026. [6] [2] Duff can remove managers and take possession of assets and records, including control of cryptographic keys. [2]

The SEC also accuses Joseph Anderson of supplying purportedly independent audit reports that falsely verified the funds' returns. [3] Prosecutors announced Williams's and Johnson's arrests on 18 November 2026; the criminal charges remain allegations. [4] The agency announcements provide no repayment timetable for investors. The Senate advances a ban on war and death contracts.

[7]

The Senate passed the DEATH BETS Act on 19 November 2026. [7] It would bar federally registered prediction-market venues from listing or clearing contracts involving war, terrorism, assassination or individual deaths. [8] An amendment preserves existing law governing other event contracts, according to co-sponsor John Curtis. [8]

The measure still needs House approval, The Block reports. [9]

Bitcoin Core cuts repeated work on transaction announcements.

[12]

Bitcoin Core released version 31.3 on 20 November 2026. [13] Its network patch filters duplicate transaction announcements, avoiding unnecessary processing. [14] The patch's author says the change should leave observable behaviour unchanged. [14]

A study measures how long an Ethereum market maker waits to learn its trades.

[15]

POL Finance researchers published a study on 19 November 2026 of proprietary automated market makers, pools priced by individual trading firms. [15] They report that El Zorro's node saw blocks a median 1.23 seconds after quotes were signed, across 404 wrapped-ether trades. [15]

The sample covers 14–15 October 2026 and one market maker. [15] The authors argue that this interval leaves the maker unable to hedge an executed trade it does not yet know about. [15]

SIPO reports that Cardano's police-evidence funding request failed.

[16]

Delegate SIPO reports that the 9.6 million ADA request for an Indian police-evidence system expired on 20 November 2026 UTC. [16] Its tally puts support at 41.59% of the eligible delegated stake, excluding abstentions, against the 67% threshold. [16] Five committee members deemed it constitutional; that finding did not supply the missing delegate support, SIPO reports. [16]

SIPO, which voted for the proposal, reports that the requested money stays in the treasury. [16]

Ethereum researchers dispute which features quantum security should displace.

[17]

Thomas Thiery argued on 17 November 2026 for retaining FOCIL, a proposed censorship-resistance mechanism, while accelerating quantum-resistant cryptography. [17] He warned that dropping agreed features could damage core developers' credibility. [17] Researcher Nerolation replied on 18 November 2026 that additional features multiply the code and interactions developers must secure. [18]

Nerolation favours narrower upgrades, with FOCIL returning after the quantum-resistant consensus design becomes clearer. [18]
