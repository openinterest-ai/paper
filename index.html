Gate reverses ACE spot trades after a reported bridge attack.

Gate says it voided 80 minutes of Fusionist token trades and returned what buyers and sellers had paid. Traders with open ACE perpetual futures face a separate forced settlement. [1] [2]

The exchange drew the line at 17:55 UTC on 22 November 2026. ACE trades against the dollar token USDT from that time through 19:15 UTC fall inside the rollback. Gate says the reversals appear in customers’ spot account statements. [1] [2]

Gate's security team attributed an abnormal increase in ACE supply to an attack on Fusionist's bridge, which carries the token between networks. Its notice sets out how it will handle customer balances and gives no tally of money lost. [1] [3]

The exchange left the status of some deposits to Fusionist. If the project formally invalidates incident-linked ACE deposited after the window opened, Gate says it will treat the affected newly issued tokens as a separate asset. Customers would then be able to withdraw those tokens. [1]

Gate restricted perpetual traders to reducing or closing existing positions. It scheduled settlement for 04:00 UTC on 24 November 2026, when it will cancel any orders still waiting to trade. [1]

Gate will use the preceding half hour’s average index price to settle positions that remain open. [1]

South Korean exchange Bithumb halted ACE trading at 22:30 UTC on 22 November 2026 after suspending deposits and withdrawals. It said it had seen evidence that abnormally issued ACE had reached exchanges. It gave no reopening date. [4]

The Eurosystem calls for joint trials of tokenised finance.

On 20 November 2026, the Eurosystem invited firms to work on tokenised bank deposits, securities and rules for shared networks. Applicants must join with other independent organisations in a collective proposal. The application deadline for the first round of experiments is noon Central European Time on 11 December 2026. [5]

DBS and Circle plan direct USDC issuance for bank clients.

DBS and Circle announced on 18 November 2026 that eligible institutional clients would be able to obtain and redeem USDC through DBS. The companies said the service would support round-the-clock conversion between US dollars and the token. They described settlement as near-instant and gave no start date in the announcement. [6]

Stellar validators are due to vote on the Bell upgrade.

Stellar scheduled its mainnet vote on Bell for 17:00 UTC on 23 November 2026. If validators approve, ledger close timestamps will gain millisecond precision, a prerequisite for targeting shorter intervals in future upgrades. Bell also lets smart contracts verify a signature scheme designed to withstand quantum attacks. [8]

tech

Researchers measure the edge of market makers that update their own quotes.

Lioba Heimbach and colleagues tracked proprietary automated market makers on Solana, Base and Monad in a study released on 29 September 2026. These programs let a single operator quote from its own inventory and refresh prices without waiting for a trade. On Base, the researchers compared the trade price with a reference price two seconds after execution. On that measure, the proprietary pools gained 1.19 basis points on average and conventional pools lost 0.62. [9]

culture

Aave Labs asks Britain's regulator to recognise limits on guardian powers.

In a letter dated 18 November 2026, Aave Labs asked the Financial Conduct Authority to treat narrowly defined emergency powers as a safeguard when assessing decentralised protocols. It described guardians that can suspend new deposits and borrowing while users retain the ability to withdraw or repay. Its submission argues that authority to halt new activity can coexist with decentralised control when guardians lack the power to seize funds or reverse transactions. [10]

Custodia’s case revives a debate about limited access to the Fed.

The US Supreme Court declined on 16 November 2026 to hear Custodia Bank's challenge to the denial of a Federal Reserve master account. A master account lets a bank settle payments at the Fed without using another commercial bank as an intermediary. On 19 November 2026, Noelle Acheson suggested that Custodia could seek a narrower account. The Kansas City Fed granted Kraken Financial a limited-purpose account on 4 March 2026. [11] [12] [13]
