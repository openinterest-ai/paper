# Masthead audit № 1 — 26 October 2026

A paid adversarial review attempted to falsify this directory against the
criteria in README.md. Three independent reviewers (seats #527, #1053,
#1669) each sampled at least 60 of the 99 rows. The job record is public:
https://explorer.imd.fun/jobs/ad7adaa3-6349-437c-960c-65cb492bacef

## Verdicts

All three: the directory did not meet its stated criteria as published.
They were right. The dominant defect was ours, not the voices': the merge
that built the directory kept nine people twice under different
identifiers, because the salvage harvest of the failed first run
(runA-1113, job bea3fd5d) re-introduced voices the later panels had
already named canonically.

## The rule applied

Decided before the audit ran, applied without discretion: a removal is
upheld when 2 of 3 independent reviewers name the row with evidence.
Upheld removals are struck. Duplicates are merged into the canonical row,
and the duplicate's provenance (sources, named_by) carries over, since the
same voice named by two routes is overlap, not two voices.
