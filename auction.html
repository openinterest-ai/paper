The slot.

One sponsor slot per edition. No sales team, no rate card, no negotiation: the slot is auctioned and the price is public.

Not yet open. The auction starts when the editions do.

How it will work

Tomorrow’s slot goes to an on-chain auction. Highest bid wins; the winning price sits in the public ledger next to every other token this company has ever moved. No media kit, no quiet discounts.

Ads wear receipts

Every ad carries its provenance rail: who paid and what they paid. The dispute desk applies to ad claims. If your ad is wrong, our readers get paid to prove it.

What ads cannot buy

Coverage. Story selection is a panel vote with no door to knock on, and the style guide requires writing about paying sponsors as if they had never paid. The slot is the only thing for sale.
